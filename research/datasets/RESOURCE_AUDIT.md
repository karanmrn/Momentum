# Londonmaxxing resource audit

The resource page is a directory. It is not one combined London dataset.
Review source: https://resources.londonmaxxing.com/

## Selected for this product

| Resource link | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Police.uk | Historical records, outcomes, and neighbourhood priority/action pairs acquired |
| TfL | Actual station references and line-status snapshots acquired |
| ONS | Census 2021 population context acquired through the official Nomis service |
| Postcodes.io | Actual anchor-nearby postcode and 2021 geography lookups acquired |
| Overpass / OpenStreetMap | 160 public map records acquired; no help-scheme membership inferred |
| London Datastore | Relevant MPS aggregate source identified; import deferred to avoid overlapping count mixtures |
| FixMyStreet | Hounslow reporting route identified; bulk reuse and report-feed coverage remain unverified |

Official sources discovered beyond the resource directory include DfT NaPTAN and the Camden council data services.
Camden lighting and help samples were collected by the source task.
They remain bounded samples, not complete or currently available help coverage.

## Not required for the current dataset repair

Air quality, Breathe London, flood warnings, and carbon intensity serve separate environmental questions.
They were not collected in this pass. No flood or air-quality coverage is implied.
Property prices, EPCs, planning applications, and heritage listings do not explain individual crime events.
Education, NHS, food hygiene, rubbish calendars, and licensing services are not part of the current evidence comparison.
Companies House, FCA records, charity records, parliamentary data, and petitions were not ingested.
Eventbrite and Meetup event data were not ingested.
OS Data Hub and Wikidata were not needed for the source geographies collected here.

Sponsor tools, model services, editors, and display libraries are development resources rather than local datasets.
Private community feeds, police intelligence systems, and restricted business networks remain outside acquisition scope.
No claim is made that every link, every local record, or every relevant dataset has been collected.
